### 1. Introduction to Facebook Lead Ads & Lead Magnet Concepts
* **What is a Lead Magnet?** Lead Magnet ek free resource ya value offer hota hai jo hum prospective customers ko offer karte hain taaki unki contact information (Name, Email, Phone Number, City) collect ki ja sake (e.g., Free Digital Marketing Starter Course, Free E-Book, ya Free Demo Classes)[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span).
* **Instant Forms vs. External Landing Pages:** 
  * Traditional lead generation mein user ad par click karke external landing page par jata tha, jahan slow mobile internet par page load hone mein 5-10 seconds lagte the aur 70% users drop-off ho jate the[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).
  * Facebook Instant Forms is problem ko solve karta hai kyunki Instant Form Facebook app ke andar hi 0.1 second mein khul jata hai aur user ki profile details (Name, Email, Phone) auto-fill ho jati hain[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).
* **NITZ Student Advantage:** Instant forms ka page load speed "Instant (0.1 Second)" hota hai aur user friction "Ultra Low" hone ki wajah se conversion volume 3x tak badh jata hai[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
